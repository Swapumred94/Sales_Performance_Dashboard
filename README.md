Multi-Tab Interactive Sales & Employee Performance Analytics Application

Project Overview
Developed a fully interactive, multi-view Excel Analytics Application to monitor multi-year sales performance (2023–2025), evaluate individual employee productivity, and track regional market distribution.

Instead of a static layout, this project utilizes an advanced dashboard architecture featuring an intuitive, application-style side navigation system. This allows stakeholders to seamlessly toggle between deep-dives into product metrics, sales team performance, and geographic market shares, significantly accelerating decision-making intervals.

Tech Stack & Key Features
Data Processing: Advanced data cleaning, text formatting, and structural synchronization across raw transaction datasets.
Analytical Architecture: Cross-functional Pivot Tables, advanced data filtering, and complex aggregation logic.
UI/UX Application Design: Fully functional side-navigation control panels, dynamic tab simulation, interactive slicing matrices (linked across multi-tab frames), custom icon formatting, and centralized color themes.

Deep-Dive Module Breakdown

1. Products Performance Module
 Core Metrics: Centralized tracking for Total Revenue (401.65M), Total Units Sold (10,013), and Total Orders (1,000).
 Granular Tracking: Features sequential line charts tracking monthly seasonal sales trends alongside ranked product categories.

 2. Sales Team Analytics Module (SALESMANS Tab)
 Performance Visibility: Tracks cross-team revenue distribution across all field agents, identifying Krishna as the Top Performer with 898 units sold.
 Actionable Ranking: Integrates specialized Top 3 and Bottom 3 horizontal bar charts to instantly spotlight top-tier conversions and flag low-performing accounts (e.g., Namita at 209 units) for management intervention.
 Segment Volume: Tracks active sales distributions reaching a dedicated team segment revenue total of 35,274,772.

 3. Regional & Contribution Analysis Module (REGION Tab)
 Geographic Market Share: Isolates revenue performance across major geographic sectors, highlighting the West (27.54%) and North (26.84%) as primary revenue drivers, followed by South (23.39%) and East (22.23%).
 Product Mix Evaluation: Integrates a comparative multi-line trend chart tracking individual performance curves alongside a centralized Product Wise % Contribution donut chart (Laptops leading at 17.97%, followed closely by Mobile at 17.73%).
